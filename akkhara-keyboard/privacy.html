Akkhara Keyboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Akkhara Keyboard ("we", "us") is a Khmer and English keyboard for iOS and Android. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. Everything you type is processed on your device. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

Who we are

Akkhara Keyboard is published by Force Fidele Kien. Contact: forcefidele@protonmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • What you type. Suggestions, autocorrection and next-word prediction run locally. Your keystrokes are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Learned and custom words. Words you add, and words the keyboard learns from your typing, are stored only on your device. You can turn learning off or erase them in the app.
  • Recorded corrections (optional). If you turn on "Record corrections", the keyboard keeps what you do with its corrections, with a few surrounding words, on your device. Nothing is sent; you export it yourself if you wish, and can delete it in the app.
  • Clipboard history (optional). If you turn it on, text you copy while the keyboard is open is kept on your device and dropped after a day. Otherwise the clipboard is read only when you tap Paste.
  • Contact names (Android, optional). If you turn on "Contact names", the keyboard reads names from your contacts to suggest them while you type. They are used on your device only.

Permissions

  • Full Access (iOS) is optional. It is used for pasting, the clipboard history, opening the Akkhara app from the keyboard, and keeping recorded corrections. Even with Full Access on, the keyboard does not send what you type anywhere.
  • Microphone is used only for voice dictation, when you tap the mic. Dictation is recognised on your device.
  • Internet is used only to download the dictation voice model, and to send dictation donations if you opt in (below).

Dictation donation (off by default)

You can choose to donate your dictations to help train a better Khmer voice model. If you turn this on, each dictated phrase is sent as audio together with the text that was inserted (including your corrections) to a server we run on Cloudflare. Clips are filed under a random identifier generated on your device, which is not linked to your name, email, or account. Donated clips wait for review before they are used for training. Dictations you cancel are never sent. You can turn this off at any time, and ask for your clips to be deleted by emailing forcefidele@protonmail.com with the code shown in the app.

Third parties

We use Cloudflare to host the dictation model download and, if you opt in, to receive donated clips. We do not sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use third-party analytics or ad services.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall Akkhara Keyboard. Donated clips are kept until you ask for them to be deleted, or until they are removed during review.

Children

Akkhara Keyboard is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy; we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, note it in the app.
